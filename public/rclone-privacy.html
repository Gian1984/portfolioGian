rclone NAS – Privacy Policy

"rclone NAS" is a private, personal tool used only by Gianluca Tiengo to back up his own Google Drive to his own home server.

It requests read-only access to Google Drive. Files are copied only to a server owned by the same person. No data is shared with, sold to or disclosed to any third party, and no other users are served.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
